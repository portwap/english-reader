Every morning, Anna takes the 7:45 train to the city. It's not a long trip — about twenty-five minutes — but she doesn't waste it. She reads.

Last year she finished 32 books. "Some were easy, some weren't," she says with a smile. Her favourite was a short novel about a lighthouse keeper who lived alone on a rock in the North Sea for 1,200 days.

Why read on a train? "Because nobody can call me there," Anna laughs. "Well, they can. I just don't answer." She thinks that reading in English every day, even for ten minutes, has helped her more than any course she's ever paid for.
